v2.11 - the next game plays without closing the app, and the sound lines up with the picture (NEW signing key)

**Install: uninstall the old NBA Hub first.** This APK is signed with a new key, so Android will not install it over v2.9 (uninstalling clears the app's saved settings).

### The next game plays without closing the app (2.11)
After watching one game, picking another (or an NBA TV channel) stayed black until the app was fully closed. Two causes, both fixed:
- the new player started on the screen before the old one had finished shutting down, and the old one's cleanup took the new one down with it; now each start waits for the last one to finish, and a quick second tap simply wins;
- every stream used the same segment addresses, which the phone caches for a minute, so the next game could be fed the previous game's video; each stream now gets its own addresses.

### The sound lines up with the picture (2.10)
On League Pass games the sound could run a whole segment (about 6 s) ahead of or behind the picture, coming and going from game to game. Picture and sound are relayed separately, and the two relays could join the live stream at different segments. Both now start at the same moment of the game (by the feed's own timestamps when it has them, otherwise by segment number). `/api/relaystats` shows how in `av_align`.

### Checked without a phone (`python tools/av_sync/av_sync_check.py`)
The app's own relay code and the real hub page, against simulated live games, played by the app's own player in Chrome:
- game switching: v2.10 passed 2 of 4 scenarios (every switch away from a game stayed black); v2.11 passes 4 of 4, including two taps 0.3 s apart;
- sound sync: v2.9 passed 2 of 6 cases (the rest off by one test segment, 2018 ms); v2.11 passes 6 of 6, worst 18 ms.

Not yet tested on a phone during a live game, and League Pass's encryption could not be simulated here.

NBAHub.exe and NBAHub-portable.zip are unchanged from v2.9 (both fixes are in the phone app; the PC relay got the same segment-address fix in the repo).
