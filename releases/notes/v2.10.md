v2.10 - the sound lines up with the picture (APK on a NEW signing key)

**Install: uninstall the old NBA Hub first.** This APK is signed with a new key, so Android will not install it over v2.9 (uninstalling clears the app's saved settings).

Fixed: on League Pass games the sound could run a whole segment (about 6 s) ahead of or behind the picture, and it came and went from game to game.

Why: these feeds keep the sound in a separate playlist, so the app relays picture and sound separately. Each relay joined the live stream on its own, and the player puts the first segment of each playlist at time 0, so whenever the two joined at different segments, the sound stayed that far off for the rest of the game.

Now both playlists start at the same moment of the game: matched by the feed's own timestamps (PROGRAM-DATE-TIME) when it has them, otherwise by segment number, and neither is played until both are ready. `/api/relaystats` shows how in `av_align`.

Checked without a live game (`python tools/av_sync/av_sync_check.py`: the app's relay code against a simulated live feed, played by the app's own shaka player in Chrome): v2.9 passed 2 of 6 cases, the rest off by 2018 ms (one 2 s test segment); v2.10 passed 6 of 6, worst 18 ms. Not yet tested on a phone during a live game.

NBAHub.exe and NBAHub-portable.zip are unchanged from v2.9 (the fix is phone-only).
